## 🔭 What I'm building

*All of it over a few months, self-taught, with AI as my pair.*

**[IAmètre](https://github.com/marionbuilds/iametre)** : measures how visible a brand is inside AI answers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Weekly automated collection, sampling with a 95% fluctuation margin, and a rule I care about: *a move that stays inside the margin is not a move.* Code and method are open.

**Smart BPJEPS, the company I've been running for four years.** It helps people prepare France's national sports-coaching diploma, a regulated qualification with a real exam to pass. In July 2026 I replaced the platforms I was renting with **SmartRoom**, a SaaS I built myself: 1,000+ students, revision content and training programs, payments, automated emails, and a gamification layer (XP, streaks, rewards) that also drives cross-selling. Its admin console is not a CRM: it crosses **Search Console and GA4 data with in-product behaviour** (how far students get in each sheet, where they stall, how they score on tests) to decide what to fix next. Next.js, Supabase, Stripe.

**Its content site, which I migrated to WordPress**: custom child theme, redirect plan, full page-parity check, without losing a single ranking. It doubles as my own SEO lab.

**Before all this: Rooride**, a carpooling app in Australia I co-founded. 5,000+ users, **all acquired organically, zero paid**, and a multilingual blog that brought **1M+ search impressions**. That's where I learned that acquisition is a product problem, not a marketing one.
